About Eagle Nation CMIS Club

Eagle Nation is a Student led club that aims to promote the boys and girls varsity sports teams here at Chiang Mai International School. We post on social media upcoming games, game results, and other resources. Support us by using our webpage when you need to know when the next game is, and following us on Instagram and Facebook.
